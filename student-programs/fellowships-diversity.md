# 🤝 Fellowships & Diversity Programs

[⬅ Back to main page](../README.md)

Programs that offer training, mentorship, scholarships, and direct pipelines into tech jobs.

## 🎓 Training & fellowship programs

| Program | What it offers |
|---|---|
| [CodePath](https://www.codepath.org/) | Free technical interview prep & courses for college students |
| [MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.io/) | Remote, internship-style program working on real open-source projects |
| [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) | Paid open-source contributions with mentors |
| [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) | Paid remote internships in open source for people underrepresented in tech |
| [Break Through Tech](https://breakthroughtech.org/) | Programs & internships for women and non-binary students |
| [Kode With Klossy](https://www.kodewithklossy.com/) | Free coding camps for young women & gender-expansive youth |

## 🌍 Diversity & community organizations

| Organization | Focus |
|---|---|
| [ColorStack](https://www.colorstack.org/) | Black and Latinx CS students — community & recruiting |
| [SEO Tech Developer](https://www.seo-usa.org/career/) | Training + internships for underrepresented students |
| [Management Leadership for Tomorrow (MLT)](https://mlt.org/) | Career prep for Black, Latinx, and Native American students |
| [Rewriting the Code](https://rewritingthecode.org/) | Women in tech community & job board |
| [AnitaB.org / Grace Hopper Celebration](https://anitab.org/) | Women in computing; huge career fair at GHC |
| [NSBE](https://www.nsbe.org/) | National Society of Black Engineers |
| [SHPE](https://shpe.org/) | Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers |
| [Out in Tech](https://outintech.com/) | LGBTQ+ tech community & mentorship |

## 💡 Tips

- Many of these have **career fairs and recruiting events** — companies hire directly from them.
- Join their **Slack/Discord communities** to hear about openings early.
