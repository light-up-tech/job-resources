# 🌱 Early Career Programs (Freshmen & Sophomores)

[⬅ Back to main page](../README.md)

These programs are designed for students **early in college** — often no prior internship needed. Names and eligibility change each year, so search the program name on the company's careers page.

## 💡 Tips

- Most of these open **September – November** and close fast.
- Your resume can include **class projects, hackathons, clubs, and personal projects** — that's what reviewers expect at this stage.
- Attend **hackathons** ([MLH](https://mlh.io/)) to build projects and meet recruiters.
