## 🌐 Job boards with good internship filters

| Resource | Why use it |
|---|---|
| [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com/) | Most universities post internships here. Log in with your school email. |
| [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) | Search "intern" + set **Experience level → Internship**. Turn on job alerts. |
| [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/) | Large volume, good for local and smaller companies. |
| [Wellfound (AngelList)](https://wellfound.com/) | Startup internships, often more hands-on. |
| [Built In](https://builtin.com/jobs) | Tech jobs by city (NYC, Boston, Chicago, etc.). |
| [USAJOBS – Students](https://www.usajobs.gov/) | U.S. government internships (Pathways program). |
| [Levels.fyi Internships](https://www.levels.fyi/internships/) | Compare intern pay across companies. |

## 🔐 By field

| Field | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Cybersecurity | [CyberSeek](https://www.cyberseek.org/), USAJOBS, defense & government contractors |
| Data / AI | Simplify tracker (Data & ML section), LinkedIn alerts for "data science intern" |
| IT / Help Desk | Your university IT department, Indeed, local hospitals & school districts |

## 💡 Tips

- Apply **within the first 1–2 weeks** a role opens — many close early.
- Use a **one-page resume** tailored with keywords from the job post.
- Ask for **referrals** from alumni on LinkedIn — they greatly increase interview chances.
