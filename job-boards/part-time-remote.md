# ⏰ Part-Time, Remote & Freelance

[⬅ Back to main page](../README.md)

Great for students who need income during the semester or want experience before an internship.

## 🏫 On-campus

- **Your university's student job portal / Handshake** — IT help desk, research assistant, lab assistant, tutoring, teaching assistant (TA).
- **Ask professors** about paid research positions — this counts as real experience on your resume.

## 🌐 Part-time & remote boards

| Resource | Why use it |
|---|---|
| [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com/) | Filter by **Part-time** and **On-campus**. |
| [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) | Filter **Job type → Part-time** and **Remote**. |
| [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/) | Lots of local part-time tech support roles. |
| [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) | Remote-only tech jobs. |
| [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/) | Remote jobs, filter by tag (dev, design, support). |
| [Wellfound (AngelList)](https://wellfound.com/) | Startups often hire part-time or contract. |

## 💻 Freelance & project experience

| Resource | Why use it |
|---|---|
| [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) | Freelance web dev, data, and design gigs. |
| [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/) | Offer small services (websites, scripts, design). |
| [Parker Dewey](https://www.parkerdewey.com/) | Paid short-term "micro-internships" for students. |
| [Extern](https://www.extern.com/) | Remote externships with real companies. |

## ⚠️ Avoid scams

- Never pay to get a job, buy equipment upfront, or deposit a check for an employer.
- Be careful with "jobs" that only talk to you over text/Telegram.
