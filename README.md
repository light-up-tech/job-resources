## 🗓️ Quick tips on timing

- **Big Tech summer internships** usually open **July – October** of the year before. Apply early — many close by November.
- **Freshman/sophomore programs** (like Google STEP) often open **September – November**.
- **New grad roles** open **August – December** of your senior year.
- **Part-time / on-campus jobs** are posted all year — check your school's job portal often.

## ✅ How to use this repo

1. Pick the section that matches where you are (first-year student, senior, career switcher…).
2. **Watch** the tracker repos listed — they update daily.
3. Keep a simple spreadsheet of where you applied, the date, and status.
4. Found something helpful that's missing? **Add it!** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
